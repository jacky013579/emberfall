Emberfall is a top-down arena survival shooter that runs offline in a desktop browser from static files. You are the warden of the last ember: hold the field against ten waves of cinders, wisps, spitters and husks, collect embers to level up and choose upgrades, dash through danger, and beat the Cinderlord on wave 10 to win — then keep going in endless mode. Four enemy kinds plus elites and a boss, a ten-card upgrade pool, synthesized WebAudio sound, particles, screen shake and hit-stop, and a best score kept in localStorage.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server --directory game 8080
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — WASD to move, mouse to aim, hold click or Space to fire, Shift or right-click to dash, P to pause.
